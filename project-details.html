Project Overview

The Brief

Nike asked us to create a motion design campaign showcasing Air Max technology. The goal was to convey innovation and comfort while preserving Nike's bold identity across digital platforms, capturing attention quickly and delivering a memorable message.

Creative Approach

We visualized air through fluid motion graphics, blending abstract particles with product cinematography. The design features dynamic camera movements, slow motion, and rhythmic editing aligned with custom sound design, ensuring each frame captivates on social media while retaining cinematic quality.

Design Philosophy

The visual system emphasizes fluidity and energy. We used a monochromatic palette with Nike's elements, making motion the main storytelling device. Particle simulations depict air molecules, while smooth transitions between product shots and visuals keep viewers engaged. Bold typography appears rhythmically with visuals, ensuring brand messaging feels integrated.
